The Ritual

Four steps, once or twice a week

  1. 01

    Apply

    Section dry hair and apply the oil directly to the scalp, working from crown to hairline.

  2. 02

    Massage

    Massage gently with fingertips for 5–10 minutes to improve circulation at the root.

  3. 03

    Leave it in

    Leave on for at least one hour, or overnight for a deeper treatment.

  4. 04

    Rinse

    Wash out thoroughly with a mild, sulphate-free shampoo.
